Pitch — for somebody aiming past the average
Posted by the owner as a participant in the Skills Economy, pitching their own trade. Anyone on the Directory may pitch theirs the same way, on Quora or in Commons, as long as it stays on the subject of the Skills Economy. That is what the place is for.

For the companion template, aimed at somebody who wants a steady ordinary level rather than an exceptional one, see pitch-the-smallest-number.md. The two are not a tier and a lesser tier. They are two people with different goals and the same arithmetic underneath.

No price appears in either. The ask is the page. A reader who meets a number before they have read the case decides on the number.

Quora

What would you charge one person, once?

Not an hour. Once. One job, one person, one time. Whatever figure you just thought of, hold it.

There are an estimated five million people living under organized targeting. One percent of that is fifty thousand. Multiply your figure by fifty thousand.

The Skills Economy has a calculator that runs this against your own trade and the sector it sits in. It takes a minute and it keeps nothing you type.

The figure is the easy part, and it is where nearly everybody stops, because a figure with nothing underneath it is just a figure. Who is the first person who pays you? What is actually standing between you and that one person — not in general, the one concrete thing? What gets done about that thing this week, and whose name is against it?

Working that out is my trade. Thirty minutes, eight questions in a fixed order, and you finish with one action and a name on it rather than five good ideas. A voice assistant runs the call; I read what comes out of it afterwards, and then there are two of us on it instead of one.

It does not end when the call does. It ends when you have a paying customer.

The economy that pushed you out has headhunters — people whose job is to pitch you, place you, and take a percentage for it. This one has nobody standing in the middle of a trade, deliberately, and that is the right way to build it. It does leave a gap: nobody is working on your side unless somebody decides to. I decided to. There is no cut of what you make.

Notes for whoever edits this next

The figure is a target with somebody's own number under it, never a prediction of income. If a line starts to read as a forecast, cut it.
No ServiceCredits in this pitch. These are dollars, about real trade with real people. The two are never set against each other and no rate between them is ever stated.
Do not reach for the caregiver, mechanic and babysitter figures from the 384-community posts. They are dollar-denominated ServiceCredits from May, they stand as record, and repeating them in current copy breaks the Dictionary's rule on what a credit is.
Do not write "psyop economy". The Dictionary carries Psyop-Free Economy for the goal and the Specterati lexicon for the actors.
Nothing about a person who has taken a call goes in a pitch. The form for writing somebody up is case-study.md, and it publishes only on recorded consent.
