Pay for a session

Seven dollars. No account, no card number, no email address. You are not asked for your name and you are not asked where you are.

Two ways to pay. Wise if you have a bank account, from anywhere in the world. An Amazon gift card if you do not — one can be bought with cash in a shop.

Paying by Wise — three steps

  1. Pick Wise below, then press Start my order.
  2. The next screen shows you a code. It is 10 characters, letters and numbers, with a dash in the middle. It also shows you the Wise name to send the money to. Copy both.
  3. Open Wise and send $7 to that name. Wise will ask you for a reference, a note or a message before you send — paste the 10-character code in there. That code is the only thing that tells us the money is yours.

Paying by Amazon gift card — two steps

  1. Buy an Amazon gift card for $7 and find its claim code. It is on the card, or in the email if you bought it online.
  2. Pick Amazon gift card below, paste that claim code in, and press Start my order. There is nothing else to type anywhere — the card code is what tells us the money is yours.

Either way, the next screen also gives you a link. Save it. It is the only way back to your order, and it is where you press the button to start your session.

In US dollars. A session is $7. Anything over that is not refunded.
Dashes and spaces do not matter.

Lost your link?

Type in the 10-character code you were given when you ordered. If you paid by gift card, type in that card's claim code instead — the same one you pasted in. Either one gets you a new link.

Nothing else can find your order. Your name, your email address and where you are were never asked for, so there is nothing else to look you up by.

There are no refunds. What you are paying for is set out on the main page, and the eight questions are published in full, so there is nothing to find out afterwards.

Your card code is destroyed once it has been checked. Nothing that identifies you is stored at any point.
